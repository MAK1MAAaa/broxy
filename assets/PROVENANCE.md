# Asset provenance

All SVG geometry and WAV audio in this directory are original project assets authored through `tools/generate_assets.py` and its `art_sprites.py`, `art_environment.py`, `art_icons.py`, and `art_projectiles.py` modules. The current visual revision is **engraved-06**. Revision 06 adds three transparent 64-by-64 icons: the hollow three-piece bone return ring, the leather blood-pact book with aged-gold seal, and the narrow soul-flame grave candle on a broad brass base. Projectile drawing revision **arsenal-atlas-v5** adds a transparent 512-by-64 SVG atlas containing eight 64-by-64 projectile-head frames, sampled at two pixels per world pixel; white cores and authored line silhouettes are baked together, with the return-ring center left clear. The asset family now contains 46 resources: 36 SVGs, including 24 inventory icons, and 10 WAVs. All 45 previous SVG and audio resources retain their exact bytes, including the engraved-05 enemies, player, scenery and existing icons. No downloaded artwork, stock samples, fonts, trained image generation models, or third-party references are embedded. The generator uses Python's standard library and a fixed random seed.

Asset dimensions, purposes, and pending engine review are recorded in `manifest.json`. Shadows and lighting are decorative and do not define collision geometry. No brands, existing game characters, or external music melodies were used.
