# Asset provenance

This document covers the legacy generated family listed in `assets/manifest.json`. The bundled font and downloaded World Flipper fan-work artwork have separate provenance and rights records in `assets/fonts/PROVENANCE.md` and `assets/worldflipper/PROVENANCE.md`; the CC0 dedication below does not apply to those third-party files.

All legacy SVG geometry and WAV audio listed in the root manifest are original project assets authored through `tools/generate_assets.py` and its `art_sprites.py`, `art_environment.py`, `art_icons.py`, `art_projectiles.py`, `art_arenas.py`, and `audio_skills.py` modules. The current visual revision is **engraved-07**. Revision 07 refines the boss aged-gold light planes and inset shadows without changing SVG geometry, alpha, canvas, pivot or draw size. Revision 06 added three transparent 64-by-64 icons: the hollow three-piece bone return ring, the leather blood-pact book with aged-gold seal, and the narrow soul-flame grave candle on a broad brass base. Projectile drawing revision **arsenal-atlas-v5** adds a transparent 512-by-64 SVG atlas containing eight 64-by-64 projectile-head frames, sampled at two pixels per world pixel; white cores and authored line silhouettes are baked together, with the return-ring center left clear. Terrain revision **biomes-01** adds three 1280-by-720 backgrounds: a green-black mire with mossy causeway, a blue-grey ice garden with fractured stone and ice spires, and a brown charcoal ashland with basalt plates and dormant fissures. Terrain ellipse positions and radii are authored once in `data/arenas.json` and read by both the SVG generator and gameplay simulation; the visible outer edge is the actual slowdown boundary. Upright scenery stays outside the fixed combat rectangle. Skill icon revision **skill-relics-01** adds two transparent 64-by-64 original vector icons: a split bone-and-steel fold clasp over plum leather, and a faceted green-grey echo stone held within separated concentric arcs. Both use the existing engraved palette, centered pivot and 24–48px inventory draw size. All 49 prior resources retain their exact bytes. Skill audio revision **skill-feedback-01** adds four short original cues: an airy dash cut, a hollow pulse, a pulse with a struck contact layer, and a double-partial evasion chime. All 51 prior resources retain their exact bytes. The asset family now contains 55 resources: 41 SVGs, including 26 inventory icons and four arena backgrounds, and 14 WAVs. No downloaded artwork, stock samples, fonts, trained image generation models, or third-party references are embedded. The generator uses Python's standard library and a fixed random seed.

The project dedicates these generated assets to the public domain under **CC0 1.0 Universal (CC0-1.0)**. Asset dimensions, purposes, and pending engine review are recorded in `manifest.json`. Shadows, lighting, floor cracks, stones and perimeter props are decorative and do not define collision geometry. Only the marked terrain ellipses apply the configured ground-movement multiplier; they are walkable and cause no damage. No brands, existing game characters, or external music melodies were used.
