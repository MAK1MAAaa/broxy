# Asset provenance

All SVG geometry and WAV audio in this directory are original project assets authored through `tools/generate_assets.py` and its `art_sprites.py`, `art_environment.py`, and `art_icons.py` modules. The current visual revision is **engraved-03**. Revision 03 refines the arena stone surfaces; the other 34 assets retain their previous geometry or audio bytes. No downloaded artwork, stock samples, fonts, trained image generation models, or third-party references are embedded. The generator uses Python's standard library and a fixed random seed.

The project dedicates these generated assets to the public domain under **CC0 1.0 Universal (CC0-1.0)**. Commercial use, modification, source redistribution, and bundling in the application are permitted; attribution is not required. License text: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode

Asset dimensions, purposes, and pending engine review are recorded in `manifest.json`. Shadows and lighting are decorative and do not define collision geometry. No brands, existing game characters, or external music melodies were used.
